Privacy Policy / 隱私政策

HVAC Toolbox Pro — version 1.0.0 · effective date: 2026

English

Data collection: HVAC Toolbox Pro does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

Local storage: Your preferences (language and theme) are stored only in your browser's local storage on your device. They never leave your device.

Network access: Network is used only to download the application once when you install or visit it. After that, all calculations run entirely offline. Transport is HTTPS-only.

Third parties: No third-party services receive any data from this application.

Contact: For any privacy question, contact the publisher through the store listing.
